PARTY ROOM — GODADDY CPANEL UPLOAD
RELEASE: 2026-10-09 · 19 games · Shared parties, host removal, and Jackbox Games

1. In cPanel, choose PHP 8.0 or newer for your domain (MultiPHP Manager or Select PHP Version).
2. Upload the latest ZIP into public_html and extract it, overwriting the existing party-room folder and its files. The ZIP contains one folder, party-room, with index.html inside. If your live folder has another name, replace its contents with the contents of party-room; do not create a second nested party-room folder.
3. Open https://YOUR-DOMAIN/party-room/ . You may rename the folder, or put its contents inside another subfolder. Keep every included file and directory together.
4. Create or join a party on the portal, or select a game first. Share the five-character code, invite link, or QR code. The host selects each game and can remove players. Return to the portal to choose another game while keeping the same party. Barcraft also supports solo practice. Tap the QR to enlarge it for a phone camera.

No Node server, build step, database, or external multiplayer account is required on GoDaddy. The eighteen live games use api/room.php on your hosting account. The portal and game screens use HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with relative URLs. Direct game URLs and invite links work from subfolders without rewrite rules.

Room storage is created automatically in party-room-data next to public_html, outside the public website. If PHP cannot create it, use cPanel File Manager to create party-room-data in your account home (next to public_html), with permissions 0700 and ownership matching your hosting account. PHP needs read/write access there. Keep this folder outside public_html. Rooms expire after six hours without activity.

Use your hosted HTTPS URL. Opening index.html by double-clicking or using hosting that does not execute PHP will not support live parties. Keep game tabs open while playing. Audio starts after a click. Voice chat is not built in: talk together or use your usual call.

To add future games: add their folder under games/, then add an entry in games.js. Paths are relative to this portal folder.

Recently shown prompts are saved in your browser and passed to new games when you host. Clearing browser storage resets that history. These are finite banks; older prompts are recycled when needed.

MOBILE PLAY
No browser extension or app is needed to play in Chrome or Safari. Party invites collapse during play on phones; tap Party & invites to reopen the code, QR, and player list. Swipe card hands sideways. Use Wavelength’s slider or -1/+1 buttons and Sudoku’s on-screen number pad. Barcraft settings collapse above the stage; transport controls remain accessible. QR codes are generated locally and point to the shared party lobby in your hosted subfolder.

UPDATING AN EXISTING SITE
After replacing the full folder, hard-refresh your browser once (Mac: Command+Shift+R; Windows: Ctrl+Shift+R). Script and stylesheet URLs include a release version, and the included .htaccess requests revalidation for future updates. If your hosting uses a separate page/CDN cache, purge that cache after upload.
To see the QR, open any live game and create or join a party. It appears next to the five-character code. On a phone during play, tap Party & invites to expand the panel. Barcraft shows a party-lobby link when playing in a party; its party QR is on that lobby.

SHARED PARTIES
Invites always open the portal party lobby. The same code works from every game. Only the host can switch games or remove a player; switching starts the selected game's lobby and resets game scores. A removed player's existing session no longer works. Returning to the portal keeps membership; Leave party explicitly disconnects. Everyone follows the host's next game choice. No additional service or server is required.

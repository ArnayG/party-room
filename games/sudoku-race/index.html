How to play Sudoku Race

Fill each row, column, and 3×3 box with digits 1–9 exactly once. Starting clues cannot change. Everyone races on the same puzzle, with private boards and shared progress. Filled-cell progress does not mean the digits are correct.

Choose a cell, then type a digit or tap the number pad. Use arrow keys to move, Backspace/Delete or 0 to erase, and N to toggle pencil notes. Notes are private. Conflicts highlight repeated digits; correctness is checked when you finish.

Each race starts with a five-second countdown. Easy puzzles have 42 clues and 10 minutes; medium has 34 and 15 minutes; hard has 28 and 20 minutes. These labels describe clue density. Every puzzle has exactly one solution. There are 450 base puzzles plus randomized digit and row/column transformations.

Finish when your board is complete. Rankings use finish time plus 30 seconds for each hint. Select an editable cell to reveal its correct digit, up to three hints per round. Solvers earn 10 points, with placement bonuses of 6, 4, and 2 for the first three adjusted times. Others earn 0. The race continues for the other players until everyone finishes/passes, time expires, or the host ends it.

Play solo practice or race up to 16 people. Choose one, three, five, or ten rounds. Your board autosaves to the room; pencil notes and unsent edits are remembered on this browser. Solutions are revealed only after the round.

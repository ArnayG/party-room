How to play Apples to Apples

Everyone receives seven red response cards. A rotating judge reveals a green adjective. Everyone else chooses the response they think the judge will like best.

Submitted cards stay private until everyone is done or two minutes pass. The judge sees shuffled anonymous responses and awards one point to their favorite. Table talk is encouraged once the responses are revealed.

The judge rotates in join order, and hands refill to seven. Played cards stay out of the draw pile until it is exhausted.

Win with 8 points for 4 players, 7 for 5, 6 for 6, 5 for 7, or 4 for 8 or more. A new player's first hand is dealt next round. The score target is fixed when the game starts.

This version uses an original red/green deck and extends the usual player count to sixteen. If someone disconnects, the host can close their missing submission or replace an absent judge after 45 seconds.

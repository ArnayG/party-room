How to play Hive Mind

The active player rolls a die and chooses one of three questions, or writes a custom question. Everyone quietly writes two to five responses in two minutes.

Every nonblank response earns one point, plus one for each other player with a matching response. Factual accuracy does not matter. Repeated meanings within your own list receive matching bonuses only once.

The die determines whether the lowest one, two, or three distinct score groups move down one level. In a three-player game, three dots become two. A star also moves the highest-scoring group up one level.

The host can merge synonyms after the reveal. Plurals are normalized automatically; use group discussion for spelling and meaning. The host applies board movement with the next-round button.

Play on a six- or eight-level hive. When anyone exits, the game ends and everyone still inside wins. If everyone exits together, everyone wins.

The digital die has faces 1, 1, 2, 2, 3, and 1★. It replaces the physical custom die. New arrivals join next round at the highest occupied hive level.

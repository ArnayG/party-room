How to play Cards Against Humanity

Every player gets ten white cards. A rotating Card Czar reads a black prompt. Everyone else submits one response, or two in order for a Pick 2 prompt.

Choose your cards in reading order, then lock the play. Submissions stay private until everyone finishes or two minutes pass. The Czar sees shuffled anonymous combinations and chooses the funniest.

The winning player earns one point. The Czar rotates in join order; hands refill to ten and used responses stay out until the draw pile is exhausted.

Choose a first-to score target or free play in the lobby. In free play, the host ends the game between rounds. These ending options replace the open-ended ending of the physical game.

These are original adult comedy cards, including profanity, innuendo, and absurd situations. This does not reproduce the official card deck. The first Czar is the host; optional gambling rules are omitted.

Disconnected players can be skipped for submission or replaced as Czar by the host after 45 seconds. The round is skipped if the Czar's two-minute timer expires.

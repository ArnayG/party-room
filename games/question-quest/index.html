How to play Question Quest

  1. Exactly one visitor is responsible. Everyone gets the same case and profiles, but your questions and remaining suspects are private.
  2. Choose a trait and ask about one value (1 credit) or a group of up to four values (2 credits). Yes/no answers automatically eliminate incompatible profiles.
  3. Naming a suspect costs 2 credits, even when wrong. Keep two credits for your final choice. Solve within five minutes to earn 20 minus credits spent. Warm-up has 16 suspects/14 credits; Classic 24/12; Expert 36/12.

The host chooses a difficulty and 1, 3, 5, or 10 rounds. Minimum two players. Late arrivals join the next round.

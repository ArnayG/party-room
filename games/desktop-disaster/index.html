How to play Desktop Disaster

One player has 90 seconds to complete a task on a simulated desktop: book a flight, find a secret file, send an email, schedule a meeting, or place an order. Follow the mission details and finish four steps.

The other players secretly control popups, notifications, error messages, or moving buttons. If there are fewer than four saboteurs, each controls several channels. Deploy a preset gadget, then wait seven seconds for it to recharge.

At most three traps can be live at once, each lasting twelve seconds. Every saboteur can deploy ten per round. Safe close/dismiss controls avoid the trap; clicking its tempting action triggers it.

Saboteurs earn one point per distraction seen by the active player, capped at four, plus two per successful trap. A hit costs four seconds; fake restart errors also move the workflow back one step.

Finish the task to earn ten points plus one per ten seconds remaining. If time expires, each saboteur gets three bonus points. Roles rotate; everyone gets two active turns in a game.

All effects stay inside the game desktop. Spectators and saboteurs see live progress, but only the active player can use the workflow. Channel ownership is revealed after the round.

Make every bar yours.

Pick a beat and a few word categories, then press Start session. Freestyle a line that includes the big word on the marked beat. The next three bars are shown alongside it so you can plan ahead.

Four-bar chains keep one rhyme sound for four words, then switch to a new sound. Try rhyming pairs for two-bar runs, or free association for unrelated prompts. Stretch mode gives you longer rhyme targets.

  1. Beginning puts your target on beat 1.
  2. Middle puts your target on beat 3.
  3. End puts your target on beat 4.

Eighth and sixteenth grids can shift targets to the &, e, or a of that beat. The timeline shows the exact spot. This is a freestyle exercise: no mic or score needed.

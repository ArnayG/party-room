How to play Word Circuit

Change the starting word into the target, one letter at a time. Every step must be a word in the built-in dictionary. Keep the same length and letter positions; no adding, removing, or rearranging letters. Plurals and inflected forms are allowed. Names, punctuation, and spaces are excluded.

Every puzzle has a verified route. Choose warm-up (4 letters), classic (4–5), or expert (5–6). Everyone gets the same pair, a five-second countdown, and three minutes. Play solo or with up to 16 friends.

A shortest solution earns 12 points. Each extra change costs 2 points; each hint costs 2. A completed route always earns at least 1. Undo or restart to improve a route; your shortest completed route stays saved. Lock it when you’re satisfied, or pass. Everyone locking ends the round.

Hints suggest a valid next step toward the target without reusing earlier words. Three hints per round. Paths stay private until reveal. The host can end the race early. Recently played pairs are avoided across sessions in the same browser.

The game dictionary contains 16,604 English words of 3–6 letters from SCOWL, including American and British spellings. It is a finite game dictionary, rather than every English word. Dictionary source · License.

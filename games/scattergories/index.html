How to play Scattergories

Every round gives the room one letter, twelve categories, and two minutes. Write one answer for each category that starts with that letter. Your sheet stays private until the timer ends or everyone submits.

Answers save as you type. Use Lock answer sheet when you’re ready. You cannot edit a locked sheet.

A unique answer earns 1 point. Matching answers in the same category earn zero. Blank answers and wrong starting letters also earn zero. A, An, and The are ignored at the start.

Talk through the revealed answers. The host can reject or accept answers that don’t fit the category; scores update immediately. Agree on reasonable answers together. Play six rounds, then compare totals.

Spelling variants and synonyms may need group discussion: automated matching ignores punctuation and case. Proper names are allowed where the category fits.

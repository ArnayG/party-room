A word-and-bluff party game. Private cards, live clues, and secret votes.

How to play Imposter

Everyone gets the same secret word except one imposter, who sees only the category. Keep your card private.

Take turns giving one related word or a short clue, without saying the secret word. The imposter tries to blend in.

After the clues, discuss and vote for another player. A tied vote or a wrong accusation lets the imposter win.

If caught, the imposter gets one final guess at the word. Each informed player earns 1 point for a catch; the imposter earns 2 for escaping or guessing correctly. Play five rounds.
